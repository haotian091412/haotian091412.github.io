Beyond Impair-Repair: Tuning-Efficient Machine Unlearning with Anti-Overrepair

TriTune reframes a coupled unlearning objective as three stage-wise, single-objective optimization problems. Adaptive anti-overrepair prevents utility repair from restoring forgotten behavior, while self-calibration reduces dependence on repeated, scenario-specific tuning.
