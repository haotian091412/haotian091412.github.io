Research

Beyond Impair-Repair: Tuning-Efficient Machine Unlearning with Anti-Overrepair

Haotian Ma et al.

Preprint, 2026

This work introduces TriTune, which decomposes a coupled machine-unlearning objective into three stage-wise, single-objective optimization problems. Adaptive anti-overrepair helps prevent utility repair from restoring forgotten behavior, while self-calibration reduces dependence on manual tuning.
